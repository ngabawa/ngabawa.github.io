CONCEPTS COVERED

This permission covers the general game-design ideas used in the project,
including:

- A life-simulation game following a character through age-based life stages.
- Choice-driven events that respond to a character's age, work, family,
  circumstances, and past decisions.
- Short, age-appropriate city activities and mini-games that use action points,
  skills, rewards, and collectible progress.
- Life-simulation systems for skills, work, family, finances, health, and
  character outcomes.
- Narrative outcomes that vary with a character's behavior and circumstances.
