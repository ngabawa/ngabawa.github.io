MYANMAR LIFE SIMULATOR — STAGE 4

INSTALLATION
1. Extract index.html, style.css and game.js into the same folder.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).
3. Keep the three files together. Do not mix them with files from older builds.
4. Back up your existing browser save before switching builds.

NEW GAMEPLAY
- City mini-games are grouped under Activities; no separate city map tab is used.
- The festival rhythm game opens at age 6, the market game at 10, and the tea-shop game at 12.
- Animated scenes, larger touch targets, sound cues, feedback, pause/resume, skill XP and collection badges make outings more tactile.
- The cash book shows the actual annual net income or expense once.
- New age- and situation-aware stories cover school, early work, parenting, elder care and retirement.
- Illnesses can include influenza, food poisoning, pneumonia, kidney disease, chronic lung disease, cardiovascular disease and cancer.
- Obituaries now explain the cause of death, including ordinary health outcomes, rare comic accidents and harsher consequences for repeated harmful behavior.
- Six persistent skill tracks: Craft, Trade, Analysis, Care, Fitness and Leadership.
- Practical decision challenges award skill XP and contextual rewards.
- Business operations include stock, quality, marketing, staffing, research and promotions.
- Annual business results respond to operations and Trade skill.

TOUCHSCREEN
Double-tap zoom is disabled, and touch controls use larger tap targets. The game respects reduced-motion settings.

SAVE COMPATIBILITY
The update advances the save schema while retaining existing save slots. Mini-game collection and story history are initialized as needed.

UPGRADE NOTES
Older saves keep their current character, skills and progress. City mini-game scores, badges and death records are added as you play.
